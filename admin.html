The sales as a cube

The sales as a table you can reshape: pick the rows and columns, click a heading to zoom in, add a filter to see one slice.

Details

The sales table is the fact table; town, street, flat type, flat model, storey and time are its dimensions. Pick what goes down the side and across the top. Click a heading to drill down into it; roll up to come back. Every view is one GROUP BY, shown underneath.

One query, with and without its index

One query, run twice: normally, and as if the database had no index. Compare the time and the pages read.

Details

Each case runs twice on the 985,219 sales: once as normal, once with the planner told to act as if no index existed. The plans, the pages read and the times are PostgreSQL's own (EXPLAIN ANALYZE).

Two transactions at once

Two jobs changing the same two bank accounts at the same moment, run for real. A red “Wrong” shows what happens when the changes are not protected.

Details

Alice and Bob have 200 dollars each. T1 moves 100 from Alice to Bob; T2 pays both 3% interest. Done one after the other, in either order, they end with 412 between them. The first four scenarios run exactly that pair of jobs; the last four use a smaller job to show one idea each. Every statement is sent to PostgreSQL on a real database session, and the page shows what it really did, including the cases where the answer comes out wrong, which is the point.

Pick a scenario above to run it.
