    From a million records to one decision

    Five steps. The panel follows as you scroll.

    1. Pick a town or flat

      Start from a town, a flat type, a budget, or just browse. Every filter maps to real resale records.

    2. See the market shape

      Charts show how prices are spread and how size drives value, so you know what normal looks like.

    3. Spot the value

      Each flat is compared with sales of the same type in the same town and period, so a 1995 sale isn’t called a bargain just because prices have risen since.

    4. Shortlist and compare

      Save the flats you like and set them side by side to make a choice you can explain.

    5. See what’s around

      Parks, hawker centres, gyms, childcare, schools and MRT stations, measured from every block. Weigh what matters to you and rank blocks by it.
