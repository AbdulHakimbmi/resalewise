How ResaleWise works

From a million records to one clear decision.

Scroll through the four steps — the panel on the right follows along.

01

Pick a town or flat

Start from a town, a flat type, a budget — or just browse. Every filter maps to real resale records.

02

See the market shape

Live charts show how prices are spread and how size drives value, so you know what "normal" looks like.

03

Spot the value

Each flat is scored against the median for its town and type — instantly flagging good deals and premiums.

04

Shortlist & compare

Save the flats you like and compare them side by side to make a confident, evidence-based choice.
